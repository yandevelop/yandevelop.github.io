# Privacy Policy for AniTime
**Effective Date:** March 24, 2026
AniTime ("the App") is a simple animated clock widget. Your privacy is
      important to us.
## 1. Data Collection
The App **does not collect, store, or transmit** any personal information, user data, or device data.
## 2. Permissions
The App requires the following permissions to function:
- **Foreground Service:** To animate the GIF characters on your home screen at a high frame rate.
- **Notifications:** To display a mandatory system notification while the service is active, as required by Android.
- **Boot Completed:** To restart the widget service automatically after you reboot your device.
The App **does not have internet access** and cannot send any data to external servers.
## 3. Children's Privacy
Since the App does not collect any data, it is safe for all age groups,
      including children under 13.
## 4. Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on
      this page.
## Contact
If you have any questions, please contact the developer via the GitHub
      repository.
